## What is Seedance 2.5?

Seedance 2.5 brings text-to-video, image-to-video, and reference-based video generation into a unified creative workflow. Creators can use natural-language prompts together with visual references to control characters, scenes, camera movement, style, motion, and overall cinematic direction.

It is suitable for creators, filmmakers, marketers, designers, and developers who want to experiment with modern AI-powered video production.

## Key Features

- **Text to Video** — Generate videos directly from natural-language prompts.
- **Image to Video** — Turn static images into dynamic AI-generated videos.
- **Reference to Video** — Use images and video clips as visual references.
- **Multimodal Generation** — Combine text, images, videos, and audio references in one workflow.
- **Up to 1080p Resolution** — Generate videos in 480p, 720p, or 1080p.
- **Up to 30-Second Videos** — Create longer AI-generated video sequences.
- **Multi-Shot Consistency** — Maintain characters, environments, and visual style across different shots.
- **Camera & Motion Control** — Guide camera movements and cinematic composition through prompts and references.
- **Scene Extension** — Extend existing scenes while preserving visual continuity.
- **Audio-Visual Synchronization** — Create video sequences with synchronized visual and audio direction.
